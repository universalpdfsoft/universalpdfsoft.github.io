2. DESKRIPSI PRODUK (LONG DESCRIPTION / OVERVIEW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stop uploading your confidential company documents, contracts, and private client files to random online web converters. 

Universal Document & Image Converter Pro is a lightning-fast, enterprise-grade desktop utility that runs 100% offline on your Windows PC. Convert, merge, split, compress, and extract documents and images in bulk with zero lag and zero privacy risks.

Key Highlights:
• 100% Offline & Private: No cloud uploads, no external server processing, and zero data tracking. Your files never leave your computer.
• 50+ Format Conversions: Convert seamlessly between PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, JPG, PNG, WEBP, TIFF, BMP, SVG, and more.
• Multi-Threaded Batch Processing: Queue hundreds of files and convert them simultaneously in seconds.
• Complete PDF Toolkit: Merge multiple documents, split specific page ranges, compress bloated file sizes, and extract high-resolution images.
• Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Extract editable text from scanned documents and photo images.
• Ultra-Lightweight: Minimal CPU and RAM footprint, starts instantly, and runs reliably on Windows 7, 8, 10, and 11.
